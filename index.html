About Me

I'm a Laravel Engineer passionate about building open source software, developer tools, communities, and content that help developers build better software. I am the author and maintainer of Pest's official PHPStan and Rector plugins.

I also work with teams on existing Laravel applications, establishing the testing architecture, static analysis, automated refactoring, and project-aware AI workflows that make codebases safer and easier to maintain.

I'm a Core Team member at Pinkary and Pest, founder of Laravel Artisans, volunteer with Laravel Ahmedabad and Laracon India.

Whether I'm contributing to open source, building developer tools, reviewing pull requests, speaking at community events, or sharing what I learn, my goal remains the same:
Help developers build better software.
